# Sennly – Weekly Expense Tracker

Sennly is a user-friendly, **responsive expense tracker web application** designed to help users organize and manage their expenses with ease. It features an intuitive drag-and-drop interface that allows users to assign expenses to specific days, save frequently used expenses for quick access, and export records as CSV files for weekly and monthly tracking.

**Designed for both mobile and desktop devices**, Sennly provides a simple and convenient way to keep track of expenses across different screen sizes.

The name **“Sennly”** was inspired by **“sennight,”** an old English word meaning *a period of seven nights or one week*. It also incorporates the developer’s nickname, **“Cin,”** making the project more personal and meaningful. The **“ly”** adds a playful touch to the name while reflecting the app's focus on organizing and tracking expenses over time, whether **weekly or monthly**. Together, **Sennly** represents a personal yet practical approach to expense management. :">

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Weekly Expense Organization** – Organize expenses by day within a weekly view.
- 📊 **Monthly Expense Tracking** – Monitor and review expenses throughout the month.
- 🖱️ **Drag-and-Drop Management** – Easily move saved expenses into specific days.
- ♻️ **Reusable Common Expenses** – Save frequently used expenses for quick access.
- ➕ **Custom Expense Management** – Add or remove expenses whenever needed.
- 📥 **CSV Export** – Export weekly or monthly expense records for reports and budgeting.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Optimized for both mobile and desktop devices.
- 💡 **User-Friendly Interface** – Enjoy a clean and straightforward expense management experience.
- 🔒 **No Login Required** – Start tracking expenses without creating an account.
- 💾 **Local Data Storage** – Automatically saves expense records locally in your browser, without requiring an account.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Create an Expense** – Tap a specific day to add a new expense, or open the side hamburger menu to access your saved common expenses.
2. **Drag and Drop** – Drag a saved common expense from the side menu into your desired day of the week.
3. **Save Common Expenses** – Add frequently used expenses to your saved list for quick access whenever needed.
4. **Manage Expenses** – Remove unnecessary expenses anytime to keep your records organized.
5. **Export Records** – Download your weekly or monthly expense records as CSV files for reporting and budgeting.
